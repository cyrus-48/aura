Orders & Shipping

Domestic orders (within the US) typically arrive within 3-5 business days. International orders can take 7-14 business days depending on the destination and customs processing. You will receive a tracking number as soon as your order ships.
Yes! We offer free carbon-neutral shipping on all domestic orders over $99. For orders under $99, a flat rate of $8 applies.
We process orders quickly to ensure fast delivery. If you need to make a change, please contact us within 2 hours of placing your order. Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be canceled or modified.

Returns & Exchanges

We offer a 45-day return policy on all unworn items with original tags attached. Returns are free within the US. Please visit our Returns Portal to initiate an exchange or return.
Simply log into your account, go to your order history, and select the item you wish to return. Follow the prompts to print your prepaid shipping label. Once we receive your return, we will process your refund or exchange within 3-5 business days.
Yes, items purchased during a final sale promotion are not eligible for return or exchange. This will be clearly marked on the product page and at checkout.

Products & Sizing

We provide detailed size guides on every product page. If you are between sizes, we generally recommend sizing up for a more relaxed fit, or sizing down for a more tailored look. Feel free to email us for specific measurements.
We recommend washing linen and organic cotton in cold water on a gentle cycle, or hand washing. Lay flat to dry or tumble dry on low. Linen gets softer with every wash. Avoid bleach and fabric softeners.

Sustainability

We exclusively use organic, recycled, and low-impact materials. Our primary fabrics include GOTS-certified organic cotton, European flax linen, Tencel™ Lyocell, and vegetable-tanned leather.
Yes! All of our packaging is 100% plastic-free. We use recycled cardboard boxes, paper tape, and compostable mailers. Even our garment bags are made from biodegradable materials.

Account & Payment

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay.
