The Beginning

A Journey Rooted
in Nature

Aura & Oak was born in 2018 from a simple frustration: it was nearly impossible to find clothing that was both beautifully designed and genuinely sustainable. Fast fashion was dominating the industry, and the environmental cost was staggering.

We started with a single, small collection of organic linen shirts, crafted by a family-run atelier we met during a trip through the countryside. The response was overwhelming. People wanted clothes that told a story, that felt good to wear, and that didn't harm the planet.

Today, we work with a network of artisans across the globe, combining traditional techniques with modern, low-impact materials. We are still small, still independent, and still guided by the same principle: less, but better.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Planet First

We use exclusively organic, recycled, and low-impact materials. Our packaging is plastic-free, and we offset 100% of our carbon emissions.

Ethical Craft

We partner directly with artisans and small-scale manufacturers who share our values. We guarantee fair wages, safe working conditions, and no child labor.

Timeless Quality

We reject the trend cycle. Our pieces are designed to be worn for decades, not seasons. We prioritize durability, comfort, and enduring style above all else.
